=== [5] How do I charge the Oral B iO Series 2? ===
1. Place the toothbrush handle on the charger.
2. Plug the charger into an electrical outlet.
3. The battery indicator light on the handle will flash while the toothbrush is charging.
4. When the toothbrush is fully charged, the battery indicator light turns off.
5. For everyday use, the handle can be stored on the plugged-in charger to maintain a full charge.
Charging tips:
1. Use only the charging unit provided with your Oral-B iO Series 2 toothbrush.
2. Keep the charger on a clean, dry surface and do not place it in water.
3. If the battery is low, recharge the toothbrush before the next use.

=== [6] How can I set a timer on Oral-B iO Series 2? ===
The iO Series 2 has a built-in 2 minute brushing timer with 30 second quadrant alerts and starts automatically when you turn the brush on. You don’t need to set it manually. Just:
Put the brush in your mouth and press the power button to start brushing.
The brush runs a 2:00 timer and gives a brief pause/stutter every 30 seconds to prompt you to move to the next quadrant.
After 2 minutes, the brush gives a longer signal to indicate that the session is finished.

=== [7] What's the difference between Oral-B Pro and iO2? ===
The primary difference between Oral-B Pro and iO2 lies in the motor/cleaning technology and the brush heads:
Motor/cleaning technology
Pro: Oral B’s traditional oscillating rotating ± pulsation action.
iO2: Combines gentle micro-vibrations with Oral-B’s oscillating-rotating action
Brush heads:
Pro: Uses standard Oral B round replacement brush heads.
iO2: Uses iO specific replacement brush heads only. Legacy Oral B brush heads are not compatible and will not fit iO handles.
Battery & charging:
Pro: Battery type, charger style, and runtime vary by model.
iO2: Rechargeable lithium-ion battery; 1 charge lasts 4 weeks and the handle recharges on the provided charging unit.
Modes:
Pro: Basic cleaning/whitening/sensitive options (depends on model).
iO2: Multiple refined modes and smarter intensity control even on Series 2.
Feel & noise:
Pro: Noticeable oscillation/pulsation sensation and somewhat louder.
iO2: Smoother and quieter.
Price:
Pro: Budget/midrange, widely available, lower cost.
iO2: Entry point to the premium iO line — higher price than Pro but lower than top iO models.

=== [8] Does the Oral-B iO Series 2 have a pressure sensor? ===
Yes. The Oral B iO Series 2 includes a pressure sensor that gives visual feedback on the handle and automatically slows down.

=== [9] Why is the Oral-B iO Series 2 recommended for people transitioning from a manual toothbrush? ===
The Oral-B iO Series 2 is an ideal entry point when transitioning from a manual toothbrush to an electric toothbrush because of the following reasons:
Gentle, smooth feel — the brush is designed to feel comfortable for new electric toothbrush users.
Built in pressure sensor — prevents over brushing by giving immediate feedback and slowing down.
Automatic 2 minute timer with 30 second quadrant alerts — teaches proper brushing duration and coverage without extra effort.
Simple, beginner friendly controls and a clear on handle display — easy to use compared with more complex models.
Effective cleaning — powered action removes more plaque than most manual brushes, improving results with minimal technique change.
Compatible compact round heads — familiar brushing motion and easy replacement.

=== [13] Warranty information ===
What is covered in my Oral-B warranty?
Your Oral-B warranty starts on the day of purchase and runs for 2 years. If your Oral-B product is defective during the warranty period, we will replace it free of charge*.
The warranty does not cover cosmetic damage such as scratches, or damage caused by:
Normal wear and tear
Accident, misuse, dirt or water
The product being tampered with
Improper use
Repairs carried out by agents not authorized by Oral-B/Braun
Failure to take proper care of the product, including recharging
The product being altered or used for commercial purposes
How can I get my Oral-B electric toothbrush serviced?
If you’re experiencing a problem with your Oral-B toothbrush, check out our Online Repair Service! There you’ll find troubleshooting tips which may help resolve the issue. Simply go to www.service.oralb.com , select your Region, Country and Language and then click on the "Online Check" button.
If you’re unable to fix the problem and need to send your toothbrush for service, you can use the online service to print off a pre-paid mailing label and get a tracking number. The tracking number will enable you to track the progress of your request. Please note you must have a receipt that includes date of purchase to get a pre-paid mailing label and your brush must be within its warranty period.
If you’re unable to use the Online Repair Service, please see the instructions below.
For In Warranty service, follow these simple steps:
Visit www.service.oralb.com to find details of your closest Oral-B Authorized Service Center.
Send the product directly to the Service Center, including:
All system components (e.g., toothbrush handle, charger, power cord, SmartGuide). You do not need to include accessories like the brush heads or refill stand.
A copy of your dated proof of purchase (always keep a copy for yourself).
A letter including the following information: your name, return mailing address, contact phone number, e-mail address and a brief explanation of the problem you're experiencing with the product.
If you include your e-mail address, the Oral-B Service center will send you updates when the product is received and when product is shipped back, along with a tracking number.
All products sent in for service must be well packaged and sent in a box suitable to withstand normal freight handling. It is recommended that your package is appropriately insured and shipped using a carrier who can provide tracking. We will cover all repair costs and return shipping to you.
For Out of Warranty service: You can still send your device to an Oral-B Authorized Service Center for service at your own cost. If the product warranty has expired, a quote will be sent to you for approval and payment, prior to any service. Before shipping your device, we recommend you call the authorized Service Center to discuss your service needs.
