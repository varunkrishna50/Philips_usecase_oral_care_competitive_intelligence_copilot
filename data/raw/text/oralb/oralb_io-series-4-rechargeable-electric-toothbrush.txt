For In Warranty service, follow these simple steps:
Visit www.service.oralb.com to find details of your closest Oral-B Authorized Service Center.
Send the product directly to the Service Center, including:
All system components (e.g., toothbrush handle, charger, power cord, SmartGuide). You do not need to include accessories like the brush heads or refill stand.
A copy of your dated proof of purchase (always keep a copy for yourself).
A letter including the following information: your name, return mailing address, contact phone number, e-mail address and a brief explanation of the problem you're experiencing with the product.
If you include your e-mail address, the Oral-B Service center will send you updates when the product is received and when product is shipped back, along with a tracking number.
All products sent in for service must be well packaged and sent in a box suitable to withstand normal freight handling. It is recommended that your package is appropriately insured and shipped using a carrier who can provide tracking. We will cover all repair costs and return shipping to you.
For Out of Warranty service: You can still send your device to an Oral-B Authorized Service Center for service at your own cost. If the product warranty has expired, a quote will be sent to you for approval and payment, prior to any service. Before shipping your device, we recommend you call the authorized Service Center to discuss your service needs.

=== [5] Why is an Oral-B iO electric toothbrush better than a manual toothbrush? ===
Oral-B electric toothbrushes are clinically proven to remove up to 100% more plaque compared to a regular manual toothbrush. All models also feature a built-in 2-minute timer, which help you brush for the dentist-recommended time. By improving your daily brushing habits and taking better care of your teeth and gums with an electric toothbrush, you reduce your risk of cavities, gingivitis, and other oral health issues.

=== [6] What makes Oral-B iO electric toothbrushes different from other electric toothbrushes? ===
Oral-B iO represents our most advanced series of electric toothbrushes, developed with dentists to deliver Oral-B’s best clean.
Oral-B iO electric toothbrushes feature a unique round brush head, powered by a magnetic system that combines oscillating & rotating movements with gentle micro-vibrations.
Inspired by professional dental tools, the round iO brush head is designed to surround each tooth, providing a deep clean while remaining gentle on gums.

=== [7] What is the difference between the different models in the iO series? ===
The Oral-B iO range offers an option for every brushing style and need.
The iO2 offers a streamlined design with essential features only for simple, effective cleaning. The iO10 is our most premium brush, designed to deliver a truly luxury brushing experience.
As you move up the range, you’ll benefit from enhanced features such as smarter feedback, more brushing modes, and greater personalisation – helping you get the most out of every brush.

=== [8] Which toothbrush head fits my Oral-B toothbrush? ===
Oral-B iO Electric Toothbrushes are compatible with Oral-B iO Toothbrush Heads only.
Earlier Oral-B models, such as Oral-B Genius, Smart, Vitality & Pro, are compatible with Oral-B Pro Toothbrush Heads only.
To ensure the best performance and a secure fit, always choose the brush head specifically designed for your toothbrush model.

Please note you must have a receipt that includes date of purchase to get a pre-paid mailing label and your brush must be within its warranty period.
If you’re unable to use the Online Repair Service, please see the instructions below.
For In Warranty service, follow these simple steps:
Visit www.service.oralb.com to find details of your closest Oral-B Authorized Service Center.
Send the product directly to the Service Center, including:
All system components (e.g., toothbrush handle, charger, power cord, SmartGuide). You do not need to include accessories like the brush heads or refill stand.
A copy of your dated proof of purchase (always keep a copy for yourself).
A letter including the following information: your name, return mailing address, contact phone number, e-mail address and a brief explanation of the problem you're experiencing with the product.
If you include your e-mail address, the Oral-B Service center will send you updates when the product is received and when product is shipped back, along with a tracking number.
All products sent in for service must be well packaged and sent in a box suitable to withstand normal freight handling. It is recommended that your package is appropriately insured and shipped using a carrier who can provide tracking. We will cover all repair costs and return shipping to you.
For Out of Warranty service: You can still send your device to an Oral-B Authorized Service Center for service at your own cost. If the product warranty has expired, a quote will be sent to you for approval and payment, prior to any service. Before shipping your device, we recommend you call the authorized Service Center to discuss your service needs.
