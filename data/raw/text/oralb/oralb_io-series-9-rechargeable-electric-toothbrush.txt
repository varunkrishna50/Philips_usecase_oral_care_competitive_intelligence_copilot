If you’re unable to use the Online Repair Service, please see the instructions below.
For In Warranty service, follow these simple steps:
Visit www.service.oralb.com to find details of your closest Oral-B Authorized Service Center.
Send the product directly to the Service Center, including:
All system components (e.g., toothbrush handle, charger, power cord, SmartGuide). You do not need to include accessories like the brush heads or refill stand.
A copy of your dated proof of purchase (always keep a copy for yourself).
A letter including the following information: your name, return mailing address, contact phone number, e-mail address and a brief explanation of the problem you're experiencing with the product.
If you include your e-mail address, the Oral-B Service center will send you updates when the product is received and when product is shipped back, along with a tracking number.
All products sent in for service must be well packaged and sent in a box suitable to withstand normal freight handling. It is recommended that your package is appropriately insured and shipped using a carrier who can provide tracking. We will cover all repair costs and return shipping to you.
For Out of Warranty service: You can still send your device to an Oral-B Authorized Service Center for service at your own cost. If the product warranty has expired, a quote will be sent to you for approval and payment, prior to any service. Before shipping your device, we recommend you call the authorized Service Center to discuss your service needs.

=== [5] How long does an Oral-B iO Series 9 battery last? ===
The Oral-B iO Series 9 uses a long-lasting rechargeable lithium-ion battery designed for extended use between charges. It also has a low-charge indicator, so you’ll know when it’s time to recharge.

=== [6] How do I change the language on Oral-B iO Series 9? ===
To change the language on your Oral-B iO Series 9, follow these steps:
Make sure your toothbrush is switched on.
On the interactive display, tap the Settings (gear) icon.
Scroll through the menu until you find the Language option.
Select the Language option and then scroll through the available languages.
Tap on your preferred language to confirm the change.

=== [7] What are the 7 brushing modes available on the Oral-B iO Series 9? ===
The 7 brushing modes available on the Oral-B iO Series 9 are Daily Clean, Whiten, Gum Care, Sensitive, Super Sensitive, Intense, and Tongue Clean.

=== [8] Does the Oral-B iO Series 9 offer 3D teeth tracking for better coverage? ===
Yes. The Oral-B iO Series 9 uses 3D Teeth Tracking to help guide whole mouth coverage.

=== [9] Can the Oral-B iO Series 9 sync with Apple Health or other fitness apps? ===
Yes, the Oral-B iO Series 9 can sync brushing data with Apple Health, but it does so through the Oral-B App. Once you pair your toothbrush with the app via Bluetooth, you can enable integration with Apple Health to automatically share your brushing statistics. However, direct syncing with other fitness apps may not be available.

=== [10] How do I turn off the Oral-B iO Series 9 electric toothbrush? ===
The Oral-B iO Series 9 doesn't feature a dedicated manual off button like some other models. Instead, it’s designed to power down automatically once you complete your brushing session or after a period of inactivity. Here are a couple of points to consider:
When you press the power button to start brushing, the brush will run for the set cycle, typically two minutes, and then automatically shut off.
If you decide to stop brushing before the cycle ends, you can usually press the power button briefly. This action will end the brushing session, and the toothbrush will then power down shortly afterward.
Alternatively, placing the toothbrush back on its charging station is a good way to ensure it’s not active.
