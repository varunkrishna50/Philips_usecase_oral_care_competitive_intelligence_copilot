Visit

A copy of your dated proof of purchase (always keep a copy for yourself).
A letter including the following information: your name, return mailing address, contact phone number, e-mail address and a brief explanation of the problem you're experiencing with the product.

Oral-B iO Series 5 Electric Toothbrush FAQs
How do I turn off the Oral-B iO Series 5 electric toothbrush?
To turn off your Oral-B iO Series 5, follow these simple steps:
Locate the power button on the handle.
Press the power button until the display and any indicator lights go off. This signals that the device is powered down.
Keep in mind that if you leave your brush idle for a period of time, it may also automatically shut off to conserve battery life.
What is the Oral-B iO 5 good for?
The Oral-B iO 5 is a smart electric toothbrush designed to elevate your daily oral care routine. Here are some key benefits and features that make it stand out:
Advanced Cleaning Action: Combining oscillating, rotating, and micro-vibrating movements, the iO 5 is engineered to remove plaque effectively from tooth surfaces and along the gumline.
Interactive Guidance: With its built-in interactive display and Bluetooth connectivity, the iO 5 provides real-time feedback on your brushing habits. It offers guidance on brushing duration, pressure, and technique, helping you improve and maintain proper oral hygiene.
Multiple Cleaning Modes: The toothbrush offers various modes (such as Daily Clean, Gum Care, and more) tailored to different needs, whether you’re looking for a gentle clean, intensive plaque removal, or specific gum care.
User-Friendly Design: The intuitive controls and sleek design of the iO 5 make it easy to use for anyone. Features like the timer and pressure sensor help promote best practices to prevent overbrushing and ensure a balanced clean across all areas.
Enhanced Gum Health: Its cleaning technology not only targets plaque but also promotes healthier gums by gently stimulating and massaging the gum tissue, which can be beneficial for those with sensitive gums or at risk for gingivitis.
How many brushing modes are available on the Oral-B iO Series 5 handle?
There are 5 Smart Brushing Modes on the iO 5: Daily Clean for everyday healthy teeth and gums, Intense Clean for tough-to-remove stains, Sensitive for people with mild tooth sensitivity, Super Sensitive for those with greater teeth sensitivity, and Whitening to make your teeth extra bright.
Does the Oral-B iO Series 5 have a pressure sensor?
Yes. The Oral-B's iO’s smart pressure sensor signals if you brush too hard, too soft, or just right for an effective and safe clean while protecting your gums.
How long does the Oral-B iO 5 battery last?
The Oral-B iO 5 has a long-lasting lithium-ion battery designed for extended use between charges. It also has a low-charge indicator, so you’ll know when it’s time to recharge.
Does the Oral-B iO Series 5 handle vibrate when it's time to switch quadrants?
Yes, the Oral-B iO Series 5 handle vibrates every 30 seconds to let you know when to switch cleaning surfaces during the dentist-recommended 2-minute brushing time.
How does the Oral-B iO Series 5 pressure sensor signal if I am brushing too lightly?
The Oral-B iO Series 5 pressure sensor signals if you brush too hard, too soft, or just right, helping guide you to the right pressure for an effective clean.
