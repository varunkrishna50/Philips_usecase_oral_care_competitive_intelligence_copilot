Okay, I have to say, I’ve been really enjoying the Philips Sonicare 4100 Power Toothbrush. I’ve been using it as an upgrade from my regular manual toothbrush, and I honestly didn’t realize how much I’d like having an electric toothbrush as part of my everyday routine.

It also takes a lot of the guesswork out of brushing. The SmarTimer lets you know when you’ve hit the recommended two minutes, while the QuadPacer helps you spend enough time on each section of your mouth. And if you’re not used to an electric toothbrush, the EasyStart feature gradually increases the power during the first 14 uses, which makes the transition a lot easier.
Another thing I love is the battery life. One full charge can last up to 14 days with regular use, so I don’t have to constantly think about charging it. There’s also a reminder that lets you know when it’s time to replace your brush head, which is nice because I’m definitely guilty of forgetting about that.
Id say the Philips Sonicare 4100 is a really nice little upgrade if you want to make your brushing routine feel more effective without making it complicated. It’s easy to use, gentle but powerful, and gives you a really clean feeling when you’re done. You gotta check it out.
