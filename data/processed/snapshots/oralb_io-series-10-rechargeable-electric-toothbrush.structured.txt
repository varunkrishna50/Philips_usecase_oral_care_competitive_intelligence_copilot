What is covered in my Oral-B warranty?
Your Oral-B warranty starts on the day of purchase and runs for 2 years. If your Oral-B product is defective during the warranty period, we will replace it free of charge*.
The warranty does not cover cosmetic damage such as scratches, or damage caused by:
Normal wear and tear
Accident, misuse, dirt or water
The product being tampered with
Improper use
Repairs carried out by agents not authorized by Oral-B/Braun
Failure to take proper care of the product, including recharging
The product being altered or used for commercial purposes
The Service Center will send you a like-for-like technical replacement, or if this is not possible a device of a higher specification. The Service Center may not be able to supply a toothbrush of the same color.
How can I get my Oral-B electric toothbrush serviced?
If you’re experiencing a problem with your Oral-B toothbrush, check out our Online Repair Service! There you’ll find troubleshooting tips which may help resolve the issue. Simply go to
www.service.oralb.com
, select your Region, Country and Language and then click on the "Online Check" button.
If you’re unable to fix the problem and need to send your toothbrush for service, you can use the online service to print off a pre-paid mailing label and get a tracking number. The tracking number will enable you to track the progress of your request.
Please note you must have a receipt that includes date of purchase to get a pre-paid mailing label and your brush must be within its warranty period.
If you’re unable to use the Online Repair Service, please see the instructions below.
For In Warranty service, follow these simple steps:
Visit
www.service.oralb.com
to find details of your closest Oral-B Authorized Service Center.
Send the product directly to the Service Center, including:
All system components (e.g., toothbrush handle, charger, power cord, SmartGuide). You do not need to include accessories like the brush heads or refill stand.
A copy of your dated proof of purchase (always keep a copy for yourself).
A letter including the following information: your name, return mailing address, contact phone number, e-mail address and a brief explanation of the problem you're experiencing with the product.
If you include your e-mail address, the Oral-B Service center will send you updates when the product is received and when product is shipped back, along with a tracking number.
All products sent in for service must be well packaged and sent in a box suitable to withstand normal freight handling. It is recommended that your package is appropriately insured and shipped using a carrier who can provide tracking. We will cover all repair costs and return shipping to you.
For Out of Warranty service: You can still send your device to an Oral-B Authorized Service Center for service at your own cost. If the product warranty has expired, a quote will be sent to you for approval and payment, prior to any service. Before shipping your device, we recommend you call the authorized Service Center to discuss your service needs.

Oral-B Brush Head FAQs
What makes the Oral-B iO Series 10 special?
The Oral B iO Series 10 represents the pinnacle of Oral B’s iO technology lineup. Here are some of the features that make the iO Series 10 special:
Live coaching is built into the charger. The iO Sense™ smart charger provides real-time guidance without needing to connect to the Oral-B app. It tracks coverage, time, and pressure as you brush and guides you across all zones of your mouth for full coverage.
Smart brushing features use position detection to track where you brush so you don’t miss any spots.
Interactive color display provides real-time feedback if you’re brushing too hard.
Mode selection makes it easy to choose between 7 different smart brushing modes including: Daily Clean for everyday healthy teeth and gums, Whiten to make your teeth brighter, Gum Care to stimulate along the gumline, Sensitive for people with mild tooth sensitivity, Super Sensitive for those with greater teeth sensitivity, Intense for tough stains, and Tongue Clean to give you that full mouth freshness.
Long-term coaching through the app provides progress tracking, brushing goals, and deeper analytics to show how your brushing routine is measuring up.
How long does the Oral-B iO Series 10 battery last?
The Oral-B iO Series 10 has a long-lasting lithium-ion battery designed for extended use between charges. It also has a low-charge indicator, so you’ll know when it’s time to recharge. Charging a low battery takes about 3 hours.
Does the Oral-B iO Series 10 charger display the time and a brushing timer?
Yes, the iO Sense™ charger displays both a clock and a brushing timer. Here’s how each works:
Clock Display: The iO Sense™ smart charger shows the current time when the toothbrush is docked and not in use. The clock is set automatically via Wi-Fi. No manual setup is required. It also adjusts for Daylight Saving Time.
Brushing Timer: During brushing, the charger switches to a countdown/elapsed timer display so you can visually track your brushing time. This works alongside the built-in 2-minute timer on the toothbrush handle (which vibrates every 30 seconds to signal a quadrant change).
What’s the difference between Oral-B iO Series 9 and 10?
The iO Sense™ smart charger: The iO Series 10’s headline feature. Six LED panels guide you through 6 mouth sextants in real time, right on the countertop, with no phone needed.
More precise pacing: The iO Series 10 divides brushing into 20-second sextant intervals (6 zones) vs. Series 9’s 30-second quadrant intervals (4 zones), for more granular coverage.
Quieter operation: The iO Series 10 runs at ~64 dB vs. ~72 dB for Series 9.
Fewer brush heads in the box: Series 9 comes with 4 heads; Series 10 includes just 1.
Does the Oral-B iO Series 10 have the same 7 brushing modes as the Series 9?
Yes. Both models have the same 7 brushing modes, including Daily Clean, Whiten, Gum Care, Sensitive, Super Sensitive, Intense, and Tongue Clean.
How does the Oral-B iO Sense base signal when I am applying too much pressure?
The Oral B iO Sense™ is equipped with sensors that detect excessive pressure during brushing. When too much force is applied, the toothbrush provides immediate feedback to help you adjust your technique. Here’s how it signals:
Visual Alert on the Display: The charging base will  show a warning with a change in color to red.
Audible cues: The brush emits a subtle audible alert along with the visual signal, reinforcing the message and prompting you to adjust your pressure.
